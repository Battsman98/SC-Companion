RSI Hangar Importer

Open SC Companion and click Import RSI Hangar. The extension processes your RSI hangar locally and sends recognized ship and vehicle names. In CCU Planner, choose Import owned CCUs to read unapplied upgrade names.

It does not send your RSI password, session cookie, full pledge pages, billing information, paints, equipment, or other extras.
